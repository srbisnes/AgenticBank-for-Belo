# AgenticBank for Belo — Investor Brief

## 1. Notice

**Independent prototype.** Not an official Belo product. No partnership or agreement with Belo exists. No real money is moved. Belo APIs are simulated.

All projections and planning figures are **planning hypotheses, not results**.

## 2. Summary

First agentic banking platform for Latin America, powered by AI and integrated with Belo’s financial infrastructure. Users do not manage transactions: they define goals; AI agents execute.

**Tagline:** From Digital Banking to Agentic Banking.

## 3. Problem

LatAm users face multiple currencies, inflation, international payments, manual financial management, complex treasury operations and fragmented tools. Current banking apps provide access; AgenticBank proposes autonomous execution.

## 4. Solution and product

Multi-agent financial operating system that analyses financial behaviour, understands goals, creates strategies, recommends actions and executes approved operations through Belo APIs (simulated in the prototype).

**What exists today (prototype in Google AI Studio):**

- Multi-currency executive dashboard (USD, USDT, BRL/PIX, ARS/CVU, MXN/SPEI, COP).
- Financial Copilot with Gemini.
- Goal Engine, Rules Engine, multi-agent orchestrator (Freelancer, Travel, Family, Treasury, Investment).
- SHA-256 audit, simulated Risk Engine, killswitch, simulated API console.
- ADF meter and projection simulator.

**What is missing:** real Belo integration, real-money execution, legal review and operational compliance, users and revenue.

## 6. Target users

## 7. Differentiation

- Goal instead of transaction.

- Verifiable audit (SHA-256).
